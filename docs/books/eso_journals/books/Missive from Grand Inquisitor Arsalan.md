Inquisitor Tashkier,  
  
You are hereby granted an Order of Assassination for Zerith-var:  
  
– Tall Cathay male  
– White fur with gray-black stripes  
– Glowing blue eyes

In the Riddle’Thar’s name,  
Grand Inquisitor Arsalan
