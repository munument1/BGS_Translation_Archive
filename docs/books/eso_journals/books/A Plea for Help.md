# A Plea for Help

ID: ext-a01a3773297691c85df3

I’m trying to reach you. Yes, you. This is harder than you can imagine, writing words to paper that doesn’t exist.  
  
White light. There’s white light all around me.  
  
Am I alive? Am I dreaming? Are these the Colored Rooms?  
  
Help me. I want to come home. Please.  
  
Darien
