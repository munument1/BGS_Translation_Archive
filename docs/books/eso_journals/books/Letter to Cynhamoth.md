# Letter to Cynhamoth

ID: ext-2ff59d3cf345fb30e2b1

Ah, fair Cynhamoth, how you flatter me with tempting gifts!  
  
You know I’m watching my waistline, but still you send me buttered scamp knuckles.  
  
(They were delicious, by the way!)  
  
—Sir Cadwell
