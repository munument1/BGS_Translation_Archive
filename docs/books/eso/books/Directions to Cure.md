# Directions to Cure

ID: ext-4d2e60e5fd34ff718895

Directions to Cure, First Clue  
  
Follow the old road above.  
  
Go south until you reach the crystal sconce.  
  
Take a left at the arch.  
  
Find the platform by the lake.  
  
Directions to Cure, Second Clue  
  
Swim north between the two pillars.  
  
Turn right to the beach.  
  
Directions to Cure, Final Clue  
  
Unearth the key from the right most mound.  
  
Enter the door at the end of the lake.
