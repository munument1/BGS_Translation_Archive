# Join the Order of the Black Worm!

ID: ext-79c4dcef5f6f7e10dedc

Fellow necromancer and follower of the dark arts.  
  
If this note has come into your possession through a reanimated crow or the ambulatory skeleton of a long-dead sloth, then I bid you salutations and congratulations! Your efforts in necromancy have attracted our notice. Hence this opportunity. To practice your skills in the service of dark powers as a member of the Order of the Black Worm.  
  
Imagine, no more need to hide, to pretend that you are something other than what you are. To work and study alongside like-minded individuals, all striving to achieve a common goal.  
  
In the order, you are assigned your own quarters, a bed to sleep on, a workspace to continue your research in necromancy, and hot meals whenever you please. As necromancers, we must make full use of the dead; for what other purpose does a corpse serve?  
  
Death is Only the Beginning  
  
We command death. It binds us and connects us. Imagine the power you of a dozen, a hundred, no, a thousand necromancers working side by side. We will be unstoppable!  
  
Will you continue to hide within a culture that once had a proud necromantic tradition but now considers it anathema, practicing your gifts in fear? Or will you join us and fully embrace your true potential as a necromancer? The choice is yours.  
  
And the Order of the Black Worm is waiting.
