# Ode to a Watchtower

ID: ext-7485d6d130f0d58ddfc0

I never thought I’d live to see  
  
A structure in more sorry shape than me.  
  
Its tumbled stone and broken crown  
  
Do nothing to alleviate my deepest frown.  
  
It stands forlorn within the wood  
  
An unfulfilled promise never made good.
