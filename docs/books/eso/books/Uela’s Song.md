# Uela’s Song

ID: ext-5c4106a6c03e2b600c35

Clouds tower  
  
Beyond peaks  
  
Storm shatters  
  
Mountainside  
  
Wind cudgels  
  
Canyon wall  
  
Avalanche  
  
Scours gorge  
  
Hunters spear  
  
Cave bears  
  
Tear the flesh  
  
Split the bones  
  
Briarhearts  
  
In the Reach  
  
Bretons hide  
  
From storm wrack  
  
Fear mountains  
  
Shiver, cold  
  
Run to town  
  
Cower, roofed  
  
Go to church  
  
On their knees  
  
Pray to be  
  
Sheltered, hid  
  
Come out when  
  
Weather’s warm  
  
Kill and eat  
  
Helpless lambs  
  
Hunters spear  
  
Soft Bretons  
  
Tear the flesh  
  
Split the bones
