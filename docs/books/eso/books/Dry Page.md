# Dry Page

ID: ext-c838aa15e53cd516dc71

When the snow returns  
  
and the rivers freeze,  
  
Sun hides like a child  
  
between its mother’s knees.  
  
The wise Nord knows  
  
that the end comes soon,  
  
He grows impatient  
  
awaiting his doom.
