# Wispheart Totem Clue

ID: ext-f810b7492e91d2271654

Wraiths are at home  
  
In this doomed, craggy land  
  
With tall, fanged spires  
  
And ghosts close at hand.  
  
At southwestern corner  
  
Of Markwasten Moor  
  
Here lies your card  
  
At Agea Relle’s door.
