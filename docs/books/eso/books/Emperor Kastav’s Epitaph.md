# Emperor Kastav’s Epitaph

ID: ext-b966eae5e05e684a0e70

Here lie the mortal remains of the Emperor Kastav, deposed yet sacrosanct, deceased annum 2812. His interment complete, I return to my quarters to take my own life.  
  
—Honor Guard Captain Sadineras-Kor
