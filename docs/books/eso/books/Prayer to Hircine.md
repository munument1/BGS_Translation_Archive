# Prayer to Hircine

ID: ext-c68e93042f52e1acd2f3

I lift my weapons to you!  
  
Bless them, and guide them true—  
  
A bow!  
  
A sword!  
  
A dagger!  
  
A claw!  
  
Let the sport begin!  
  
Let the blood spill!  
  
Hunter and hunted,  
  
In a chase to the death!
