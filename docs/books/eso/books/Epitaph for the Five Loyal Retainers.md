# Epitaph for the Five Loyal Retainers

ID: ext-3b358ef37874b252b0b5

King Dynar’s five loyal retainers remain ever vigilant after walking the path of the Emblem  
  
Lor-Emero started in the extreme west  
  
Parhelia followed, riding east for four nights  
  
Ballepelin was next, moving north for three days  
  
Yondvarla came fourth, traveling west for two nights  
  
The Unnamed was last, taking one step north before unleashing the preserving mist
