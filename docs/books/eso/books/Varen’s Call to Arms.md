# Varen’s Call to Arms

ID: ext-ea5c6c7c06c5083e1683

To the esteemed Weatherleah Family,  
  
Our so-called emperor, the blasphemer Leovic, has gone too far. His legalization of Daedra worship is an outrageous act of tyranny that no honorable Colovian should bow to.  
  
Duke Varen Aquilarios of Chorrol beseeches all able-bodied fighters of the Colovian Estates to join in his march against the emperor.  
  
Should you have a fire in your heart and loyalty in your blood, join our growing liberation forces in Bruma.  
  
Fight for Colovia. Fight for honor. Fight to restore glory to the Empire.
