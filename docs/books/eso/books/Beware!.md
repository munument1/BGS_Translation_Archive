# Beware!

ID: ext-71850303299da2d16a44

Climb the hill,  
  
Unlatch the door.  
  
Therein you shall find  
  
Rare goods galore!  
  
Whirring and hissing  
  
And squeaking abound.  
  
Could those be rats,  
  
Or gears you have found?  
  
Use items with care  
  
In this noisy place,  
  
Lest Dwemer awaken  
  
And give you good chase!
