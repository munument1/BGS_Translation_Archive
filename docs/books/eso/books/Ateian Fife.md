# Ateian Fife

ID: ext-c8bde638c3b7550b52eb

One of the last flutes crafted by the legendary Imperial flutesmith, Parteron Ateia. Parteron’s flutes were rare and coveted. This is the last one he finished working on before he died, which he specifically donated to the Bards College to be enjoyed and preserved for the centuries to come.  
  
Though some of his fifes are still used around Tamriel, most are kept hidden away by collectors or the supremely talented flautists within the Ateia family.
