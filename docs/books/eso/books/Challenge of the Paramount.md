# Challenge of the Paramount

ID: ext-4ca2164b36d1bbe2fd25

The Paramount has come to the Telvanni Peninsula in pursuit of sport!  
  
A favored son of Vivec and erstwhile supplicant of the Mother of Mournold, the Paramount proffers notoriety and wealth to any that bests him in a game of wits, daring, and grace—Tales of Tribute!  
  
If you seek a challenge, you will find the Paramount waiting at Three-Waters Meld in the heart of the Telvanni Peninsula.
