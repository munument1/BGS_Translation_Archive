# Challenge of the Paladin

ID: ext-8d26f8daed0ffcccf96c

A champion of the Eight awaits you, if you seek a challenge in that most storied of pass times: Tales of Tribute.  
  
The templar and scholar of Alessian legend Caledonia Gregorius is on a pilgrimage across this war-torn land. This godly woman now finds herself temporarily sequestered in Broken Promises cave. Do you wish to test your mettle against her skill and cunning?  
  
If so, seek her out beyond the North Hook where the scarps of Hammerfell come down to meet the plains of the weald. And may the Eight show you mercy, for in her deck you will find none.
