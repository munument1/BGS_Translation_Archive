# Ode to a Brass Lily

ID: ext-9527fcb59ca571dc61d3

A brass lily nods under gentle showers,  
  
Its filigreed petals polished by nature’s caress,  
  
So too do you blush under a rain of kisses,  
  
Your gentle sighs put its grace to shame.
