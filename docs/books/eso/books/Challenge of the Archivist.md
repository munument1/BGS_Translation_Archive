# Challenge of the Archivist

ID: ext-7598ec49eb2aca91de13

Take notice, Tribute players!  
  
The apocryphal scholar Master Metrand has come to Craglorn, and his enigmatic deck representing the Prince of Fate demands your attention.  
  
A student of far-flung cultures and librarian of esoterica, he promises fame and fortune to any that bests him in a game of wit, daring, and grace:  
  
Tales of Tribute!  
  
If you seek a challenge, find Master Metrand beneath the headless statue overlooking the Seeker’s Archive in Craglorn.
