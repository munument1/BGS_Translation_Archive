# Charge of Shor

ID: ext-308d3d431b20607855f6

When the Aedra formed our realms from creatia, did Shor abandon his creations? No.  
  
When the foreign gods doomed Shor to the underworld, did he go quietly? No.  
  
When the sun threatened to still beyond the horizon, did Shor leave us in the darkness? No.  
  
When all seems dark, spark a fire.  
  
When others seek to decide your fate, stand firm.  
  
When all else fails, do not go to Sovngarde without a fight.  
  
This is Shor’s charge. This is what makes Shor’s Stand.  
  
Frogirde
