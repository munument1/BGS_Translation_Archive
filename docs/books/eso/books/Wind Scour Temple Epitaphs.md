# Wind Scour Temple Epitaphs

ID: ext-a1fb61e4f2d9b8000540

Grandmaster Ghelin-Brol (1E 2703 – 2734)  
  
First of the Grandmasters, laid to rest  
Appointed by the Son of the West  
We honor him with brazier bright  
May he watch over our endless fight  
  
Grandmaster Vashu-Pir (1E 2734 – 2758)  
  
He who honored Akatosh’s light  
Who fought against the black beast’s blight  
Is the second we praise by fire and prayer  
As we honor the oath the Dragonguard share  
  
Grandmaster Jaiv-Yora (1E 2808 – 2835)  
  
She who oversaw Alduin’s Wall  
That recorded the Dragon’ rise and fall  
We honor her, this third Grandmaster  
Who led us through victory and disaster  
  
Grandmaster Ettiene Volusus (1E 2879 – 2902)  
  
The chronicler of our sacred pursuit  
Honored as modest, honest, and astute  
She is the last Grandmaster that we praise  
By prayer and faith and brazier’s blaze
