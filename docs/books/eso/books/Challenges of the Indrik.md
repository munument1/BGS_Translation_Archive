# Challenges of the Indrik

ID: ext-10eec60f02d00c0b9180

Challenge of the Anchor  
  
Vile Daedric creations fall from the skies. Scourge their presence from the land known as Auridon.  
  
Challenge of Teamwork  
  
With fellow heroes by your side, face off against other champions seeking glory.  
  
Challenge of Strength  
  
Vile beings of superior strength await your challenge in the delves of Auridon. Slay them. Prove you are stronger.  
  
Challenge of Cleansing  
  
The unliving rise and sully the purity of the land of Auridon. Use your strength to send the dead back to their domain.
