# Chronicles of Kinlord Orlemar, Part IV

ID: ext-95ba800ed3c761edc74b

An account of the second Corelanya kinlord to reign in Sunport.  
  
1E 1089: The Studies of the Kinlord  
  
Kinlord Orlemar studied Lirunalion’s accounts of Kinheir Iniel’s earlier expeditions to Mor Naril at length and spent some time retracing her steps. Mindful of the island Argonians’ superstitions regarding the Daedric ruin, Orlemar left the central temple undisturbed. However, he brought away many curious relics, including crystal eyes from the large Argonian statues that stand watch over the ruins. It is said the statues once came to life to battle whatever inhabited the temple, but the eyes are mere curiosities now.  
  
1E 1107: Vaedinhill Manor  
  
The kinlord established his new countryside home at Vaedinhill, building a fine manor overlooking the forests of eastern Solstice that was far from the politics of Sunport. Here he arranged his library and collection of mystic treasures and relics, making it the wonder of all Solstice. Many of the more valuable relics, such as the Eyes of the Colossal, Kinlord Orlemar secured in a great vault beneath Vaedinhill Manor. That vault, said to be impenetrable, held his treasures until such time as he decided to display them to scholars, nobles, and other interested parties.
