# Prayer to My Prince

ID: ext-db4528600fd310decc24

These gifts are meager,  
  
But I beseech you!  
  
Guide my actions,  
  
Hear my thoughts  
  
Across the ages, and  
  
Through the realms  
  
I approach the altar,  
  
My heart full of  
  
Devotion and love  
  
For my Prince!
