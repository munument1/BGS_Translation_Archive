# The Call Beyond

ID: ext-880c9985d5a2593c98df

Translated by Solis Aduro  
  
Come children, gather  
  
The sun is setting  
  
And you must sleep  
  
Fear not for your roots  
  
I will keep them  
  
And so when you wake  
  
You will remember what I have done
