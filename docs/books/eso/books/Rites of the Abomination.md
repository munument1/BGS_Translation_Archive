# Rites of the Abomination

ID: ext-789ffb602443cfcf4971

Thorn and blood.  
  
The briarheart’s limbs sway.  
  
Feather and fear.  
  
The briarheart’s limbs awaken.  
  
Bone and claw.  
  
The briarheart’s limbs kill.  
  
Die, briarheart, and lurch to life again.
