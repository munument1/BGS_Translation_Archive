# The Sea Stone

ID: ext-06c3e3db19465a74977a

According to legends, this ancient vessel was provided and crewed by Stonelore Circle druids and sailed into battle with the All Flags Navy when the fleet assaulted Thras. After it returned to High Isle, Archdruid Orlinan donated it to put on display as part of the memorial.
