# Song of the Spirits

ID: ext-244d59aede689ccbfb69

The night is dark,  
  
The ground is deep,  
  
Its warmth can keep you still.  
  
Your pain forget,  
  
Your anguish gone,  
  
Your slumber will not end.  
  
I am your guard,  
  
I am your hope,  
  
I will not fail my charge.  
  
The night is dark,  
  
The ground is deep,  
  
You shall not rise today.
