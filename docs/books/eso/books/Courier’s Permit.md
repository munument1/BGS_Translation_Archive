# Courier’s Permit

ID: ext-b9cccc4e3bddae0182a6

BY ORDER OF THE MAGES GUILD, this document gives:  
  
BELVIS SALAVEL  
  
Courier for the Antiquarian Circle  
  
express permission for fair and free travel across all lands, holds, and borders that have granted access to members of the Mages Guild.  
  
The holder of this document agrees to be responsible for any fees incurred during travel, including but not limited to tolls, permits, bribes, meals, hospitality, insurance, ransoms, gambling losses, and robberies.  
  
In the event that an antiquity is damaged during transport, the antiquity’s value in gold will be deducted from the courier’s pay.  
  
The holder of this document is a representative acting in the interest of the Mages Guild and the Antiquarian Circle, but does not serve to confirm membership in either party. If found, please return to the closest Mages Guildhall.  
  
THIS DOCUMENT OFFERS NO PROMISE OF PAYMENT IN THE EVENT THAT THE HOLDER IS FOUND DECEASED.  
  
Signed,  
  
Vanus Galerion, Mages Guild Founder and Archmage
