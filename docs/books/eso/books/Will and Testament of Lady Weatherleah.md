# Will and Testament of Lady Weatherleah

ID: ext-54536671bbd9514fa4e0

I, Lady Lucilla Weatherleah of Weatherleah Estate, being of sound mind and body, do make the following determination for my remaining wealth and holdings upon the occurrence of my death. A trusted recipient will understand.  
  
Weatherleah’s walls hide a glittering secret.  
  
Safely guarded by four loyal paws.
