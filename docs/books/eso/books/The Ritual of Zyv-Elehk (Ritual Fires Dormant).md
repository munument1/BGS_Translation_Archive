# The Ritual of Zyv-Elehk (Ritual Fires Dormant)

ID: ext-d28be37a951169dc0faa

The fires remain cold.  
  
But do not rest easy, blood of my blood.  
  
Ghishzor will come again to collect. If not today, some day soon. Be ready.
