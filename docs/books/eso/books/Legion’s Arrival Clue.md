Saint Pelin rests unending  
  
Dead men kneel in furtive prayer  
  
Or scrape and groan defending  
  
‘Neath the watchful gaze of Saints  
  
Where stands a matching pair  
  
Player, here lies your card  
  
As Legion trumpets blare
