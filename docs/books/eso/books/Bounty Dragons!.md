# Bounty: Dragons!

ID: ext-122957bd9536dc288501

Take heed, would-be Dragonslayers!  
  
A rage of Dragons burns its way across the Khajiiti homeland of Elsweyr! The Mane beseeches all who would lend aid in this dark time to pledge their swords to the destruction of the fell beasts!  
  
Slay a Dragon, and bring a token of proof to Battlereeve Tanerline‎‎ in Rimmen or Chizbari the Chipper in Senchal for your reward!
